# Discovery / Scoping Questionnaire

Run during qualification (proposal step 1). Answers drive scope, effort, and win theme.

## Driver & context
- What triggered this initiative? (incident, audit, regulation, insurer, board, digitalization)
- What outcome defines success in 12 months?
- Any regulatory deadline or contractual obligation?

## Environment
- Number of sites in scope? Geographic spread?
- Sectors/processes (power, O&G, water, manufacturing, F&B, ...)?
- Approximate asset count per site? Key vendors (Siemens, Rockwell, Schneider, Emerson, Honeywell, Triconex/HIMA)?
- Network segmentation state — flat, partially segmented, Purdue-aligned?
- SPAN/TAP / mirror capability available?
- Existing security tooling (NDR, SIEM, EDR, firewalls)?

## Maturity (map to the 00 model)
- Do you have an OT asset inventory today? How current?
- Any OT network visibility today (NDR)?
- Any OT detections/monitoring today, or IT SOC only?
- Existing IR plan covering OT?

## Safety & operations
- Safety-instrumented systems present? Vendor?
- Is passive/read-only monitoring acceptable? MOC process in place?
- Who authorizes actions on OT assets? Is operations/engineering available for investigations?

## Delivery model & constraints
- Internal build, full MSS, or co-managed?
- Monitoring hours required — 8x5 or 24x7?
- Change-window constraints for deployment?
- Which pre-deployment documents exist? (architecture, Purdue, comms matrix, firewall, P&ID, PLC I/O, historian tags, risk assessment)

## Commercial
- Budget range / funding model (capex vs opex)?
- Procurement route and timeline?
- Decision-makers and evaluation criteria?
