# 01 — Business Case

Frame this as a **growth and differentiation** initiative, not a technical project. Leadership funds services that win clients and defend margin.

## The opportunity
OT/ICS security is one of the fastest-growing segments of managed security, and most MSS providers still offer only repurposed IT monitoring. An OT-specific MDR/monitoring service is a differentiator that:
- Opens new buyers (asset owners in critical infrastructure) and larger deals (OT + IT convergence).
- Deepens existing accounts (many current IT clients also run OT they can't monitor).
- Commands premium pricing (specialized, scarce capability).

## Why now (demand drivers)
- **Threats with physical impact** — TRITON (safety systems), Colonial Pipeline (operational shutdown), Oldsmar (setpoint manipulation), Ukraine grid. OT is targeted, and boards know it.
- **Regulation is forcing it** — IEC 62443, NIST 800-82; NIS2 (EU), NERC CIP, TSA; and in the GCC, **UAE Information Assurance (IA) / NESA** and **KSA NCA (ECC/OTCC)** increasingly mandate OT security and incident reporting.
- **IT/OT convergence & digitalization** — remote access, IIoT, and cloud analytics have erased the air gap; clients have new exposure and no visibility.
- **Insurance** — cyber insurers now require OT controls.

## Target market (adapt to your region)
- Sectors: **oil & gas, power/utilities, water, manufacturing, transport** — in the GCC especially O&G and utilities.
- Buyer: asset owners/operators; the driver is usually safety, regulatory deadline, or a post-incident/board mandate.
- Deal shape: co-managed OT monitoring (asset owner keeps operations/response authority; you bring detection, tooling, 24/7).

## Why us (differentiation to build)
- **OT-specific, not repurposed IT** — the credibility that wins with plant engineers.
- **Physics-layer & safety-system detection** — historian baseline/deviation and SIS monitoring that few competitors offer.
- **Prebuilt, ATT&CK-mapped detection content** — faster time-to-value, deeper coverage than a from-scratch build.
- **End-to-end** — assessment through 24/7 run, with OT-safe IR.
- Leverage of the **existing MSS platform** (SIEM/SOAR/shift model) — lower incremental cost than a standalone build.

## The ask (headline)
Fund a phased initiative — **assess → define → pilot → scale** — with a go/no-go decision gate after a low-cost pilot, so the larger investment is conditional on proven results. (Detail in `05` and `06`.)

## Cost of not acting
Competitors are already moving into OT MDR; clients with OT exposure will buy it from someone. Not offering it risks losing convergence deals and looking behind on a capability regulators are pushing. The downside of a small, staged bet is low; the downside of ceding the segment is not.
