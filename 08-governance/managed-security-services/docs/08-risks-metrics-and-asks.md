# 08 — Risks, Success Metrics & What's Needed

## Risk register (top risks + mitigations)
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Skills scarcity / slow hiring | Train the nucleus early; anchor hire; partner for surge |
| Wrong platform choice | Scored selection (`03`); pilot before committing at scale |
| Low market demand / long sales cycles | Validate demand in Phase 0; start with existing clients' OT exposure |
| Under-estimating deployment effort (SPAN/TAP, on-site) | Price honestly; partner with SIs; pilot to learn real effort |
| Treating OT like IT (credibility loss) | Bake in OT doctrine; OT-specific content; anchor hire |
| Investment before proof | Staged plan with a pilot gate — big spend is conditional |
| Internal misalignment (sales/delivery) | Coalition-building early; sponsor mandate |

## Success metrics
- **Phase gates met** on time (Assess → Define → Pilot → Launch).
- **Pilot success criteria** achieved (see `07`).
- **Time-to-first-value** in deployments (quick wins live early).
- **Pipeline & first client(s)** at launch; then ARR growth and account expansion.
- **Service KPIs** once running: FP rate, MTTD/MTTR, detection coverage, client satisfaction, renewal rate.

## What's needed from the sponsor
- **Executive sponsorship** — a named senior owner of the P&L conversation.
- **Staged funding** — Phase 0–1 effort, then platform/hire spend at the gate.
- **Mandate for cross-functional support** — sales/pre-sales, delivery/ops, existing SOC leadership.
- **Decision authority** at each gate.

## What's needed from you (initiative owner)
- **Own it end-to-end** — the differentiator is usually the person who drives it from idea to funded program.
- **Build the business case** (market, drivers, investment, ramp) — numbers, not enthusiasm.
- **Build the coalition** — get sales, delivery, and IT SOC leadership aligned early.
- **Bring credibility** — certs, hands-on OT detection work, and a **working proof-of-concept you can demo** (worth more than any document).
- **Run the pilot** and produce the artifacts (reference architecture, detection content, case study).
- **Persistence** — carry it across the gap between "good idea" and "funded program."

## The one-line close
Fund a small, staged bet with a pilot gate; let the pilot prove a scarce, in-demand, premium capability before we scale — low downside, differentiated upside.
