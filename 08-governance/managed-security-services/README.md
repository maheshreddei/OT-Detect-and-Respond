# Building an OT Capability Inside an MSS

**A ready-to-use plan for proposing, justifying, and standing up an OT/ICS security service inside an existing Managed Security Service.** Not a greenfield SOC build — a capability-maturity and go-to-market initiative layered on infrastructure you already have.

## What this is
A complete internal proposal + delivery plan you can adapt and take to leadership: the business case, a discovery/assessment framework, the service and platform decisions, the skills plan, a phased implementation plan with a pilot, financials/pricing, and the risks/metrics/asks that get it funded.

Companion presentation deck: `OT_MSS_Capability_Proposal.pptx` (leadership-ready).

## How to use it
1. Run the **discovery** ([`docs/02`](docs/02-discovery-framework.md)) to establish your current state honestly.
2. Shape the **business case** ([`docs/01`](docs/01-business-case.md)) around your market and drivers.
3. Decide the **service & platform** ([`docs/03`](docs/03-service-and-platform.md)) and **skills** ([`docs/04`](docs/04-skills-and-team.md)) approach.
4. Present the **phased plan** ([`docs/05`](docs/05-implementation-plan.md)) with a **pilot** ([`docs/07`](docs/07-pilot-plan.md)) as the decision gate.
5. Bring the **financials** ([`docs/06`](docs/06-financials-and-pricing.md)) and the **asks** ([`docs/08`](docs/08-risks-metrics-and-asks.md)).

## The core message
The hardest part isn't the OT technical content — it's the **commercial and organizational** work: proving the market, securing investment, and aligning sales and delivery. Lead with the business case and a pilot; let technical depth be the proof, not the pitch.
